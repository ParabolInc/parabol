## How to read this table

Status is one of Not started, Discovery, Planned, In progress, At risk or Shipped. An initiative is At risk when the owner no longer trusts the quarter, even if work is ongoing.

Anything not on this page is not planned. Send requests to Beatrix with the customer problem, not the solution.
