# Feature launch checklist

Use this list for every customer-facing feature. Copy it into the launch ticket and tick items as they are finished.

- [ ] Product requirements are approved by the product lead
- [ ] Feature flag exists and is off by default
- [ ] Help center article is drafted and reviewed by support
- [ ] Support has been briefed and the macros are ready
- [ ] Finance has reviewed pricing and billing changes
- [ ] Analytics events are verified in staging
- [ ] Rollout plan is written: 5%, then 25%, then everyone
- [ ] Announcement email and in-app banner are scheduled
- [ ] A post-launch review is booked for two weeks after launch
